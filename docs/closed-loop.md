# Closed-Loop Analytics

## 1. Objective

The Closed-Loop Analytics module evaluates the performance of
decisions recommended by the Supply Prescript optimization engine.

The system compares the expected outcome of a recommendation with
the actual outcome after the decision is implemented.

---

## 2. Closed-Loop Workflow

The Supply Prescript closed-loop process is:

Data
↓
Prediction
↓
Optimization
↓
Prescription
↓
Decision
↓
Actual Action
↓
Actual Outcome
↓
Outcome Evaluation
↓
Analytics KPIs
↓
ROI Analysis
↓
Feedback

---

## 3. Expected Outcome

Before implementing a recommendation, the system stores expected
values such as:

- Expected Cost
- Expected Delay
- Expected Inventory
- Expected Service Level

---

## 4. Actual Outcome

After the decision is implemented, the system records:

- Actual Cost
- Actual Delay
- Actual Inventory
- Actual Service Level

---

## 5. Outcome Metrics

### Cost Variance

Cost Variance = Actual Cost - Expected Cost

A negative value means that the actual cost was lower than expected.

### Cost Savings

Cost Savings = Expected Cost - Actual Cost

A positive value represents money saved.

### Delay Variance

Delay Variance = Actual Delay - Expected Delay

A negative value means that the actual delay was lower than expected.

---

## 6. ROI

ROI is calculated as:

ROI (%) = (Cost Savings / Expected Cost) × 100

---

## 7. Decision Success

A decision is initially considered successful when:

Actual Cost <= Expected Cost

AND

Actual Delay <= Expected Delay

---

## 8. Analytics KPIs

### Cost KPIs

- Expected Cost
- Actual Cost
- Cost Variance
- Cost Savings
- Cost Reduction Percentage

### Delay KPIs

- Expected Delay
- Actual Delay
- Delay Variance
- Delay Improvement Percentage

### Decision KPIs

- Total Decisions
- Successful Decisions
- Failed Decisions
- Decision Success Rate

### ROI KPIs

- Total Savings
- Average ROI
- ROI Percentage

---

## 9. ROI Dashboard

The ROI dashboard will display:

1. Total Savings
2. Total Decisions
3. Average ROI
4. Decision Success Rate
5. Expected vs Actual Cost
6. ROI Trend
7. Decision Success Distribution

---

## 10. Closed-Loop Goal

The goal is to continuously compare:

Expected Outcome vs Actual Outcome

The comparison helps determine whether the recommendation was
successful and provides analytics that can be used for future
decision-making.
